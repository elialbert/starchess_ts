    How starchess works
    • Setup. Each side has five pawns and a king, queen, rook, bishop and knight on its back row. Every game draws a random arrangement for white, and black gets its mirror image. Arrangements that lose by force are left out.
    • Pawns step forward, can step twice from the start, take one space diagonally forward, and promote on the enemy's back row.
    • Bishops slide along the four diagonals; queens along all six lines.
    • Rooks slide straight up and down, and can also take one quiet step diagonally.
    • Knights jump two along a line and one to the side, or one along and two to the side.
    • Kings step one space. Checkmate wins; stalemate, threefold repetition and 50 quiet moves are draws.

    To move, click a piece and then where it should go. Keys: space pauses, → steps, n starts a new game, u takes back a move.
